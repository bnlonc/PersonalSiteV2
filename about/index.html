Hi! 👋 I'm Ben.

I'm a software development engineer located in Seattle. I graduated from UC San Diego with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science in 2023.

I was admitted as a National Merit Scholar and was awarded the Shores Scholarship, the highest-valued merit-based scholarship available at UCSD. While there, I was a student government officer for three years, Vice President of the Kendo Club at UCSD for one year, and President of the Kendo Club at UCSD for one year.

While in college, I interned at Amazon twice. After graduation, I returned to Amazon, where I currently develop AWS-based services that monitor and secure diverse third-party cloud environments.

I'm an Extra-class ham radio operator, electronics hobbyist, amateur guitar player, and music collector.

I have achieved the rank of kendo 2-dan and have had the privilege of practicing with:

  • The Kendo Club at UC San Diego
  • San Diego Kendo Bu
  • The Kendo Club at the University of Washington
  • Columbia Kendo Club (South Carolina)
